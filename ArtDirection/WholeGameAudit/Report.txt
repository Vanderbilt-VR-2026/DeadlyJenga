Demo bulk loadouts and whole-game regression audit - 2026-10-24

USE
Open the running Demo menu: Play > Give weapons / powerups.
Choose Target: ALL PLAYERS + BOTS, choose THIS LIFE ONLY or NOW + EVERY RESPAWN,
then scroll down to choose an item. The same options exist in the world-space VR
Powerups > Give equipment page. Stop respawn grants disables future grants while
leaving currently held equipment intact. Commands require the Demo host.

BEHAVIOR
Living survivor players and regular bots receive the chosen item immediately.
Every-respawn mode also covers new survivor arrivals. Enemy NPCs and giant hands
are excluded. One inventory slot is retained. Bulk grants replace inventory
without generating old-item pickup piles; deaths still drop held equipment.
A mid-life pickup or swap is not overwritten by the respawn policy. Timed powers
retain their normal lifetime. Current-life grants disable the previous automatic
policy. Demo round restarts retain the policy; changing to competitive modes
clears it. Snapshots preserve and validate the policy across authority restore.
