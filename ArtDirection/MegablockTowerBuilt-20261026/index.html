Megablock Tower

A single enormous enclosed block tower with real windows, broad internal switchbacks, rotating side-room layouts and a walkable roof. These are actual Unity captures of the built map.

Built and validated in the dedicated slot-04 worktree. Integration into the primary project is pending the pool's safe integration window.

Four independent windowed corners per floor, one supported stair section per floor, four roof quarters and two entrance ramps. Components within each section stay connected. The structure has no fixed core, joints or magnetic supports. Small bevelled landing noses let the player cross independently settled sections.

Each test begins from a rebuilt tower. These show the tested forces and support conditions; they do not promise that every disaster will topple this structure.

Validation

The production survivor motor walked all 122 waypoints from the island to the roof without teleports between flights. Gravity settling, all 30 floors' window collision, wall climbing and moving-anchor grip, seeded vault queries on this map and a legacy block, 32-bot stress, physical impact, support-loss collapse and reset passed with no recorded runtime errors.

Frame samples below are from a shared-machine, graphics-enabled batch Editor run. Evidence cameras are rendered manually. These numbers are not standalone gameplay or Quest benchmarks. Headset comfort and multiplayer were not tested.
