Twenty towers, two real Unity islands

Actual Unity screenshots and native PhysX evidence. The production before/after pairs use matching viewpoints. The isolated impact captures use a 22 m/s X + 9 m/s Z off-centre pulse, following any separately logged support release. Production demolition resets the intact map first. Both are separate experiments from the normal tsunami.

Large fabricated sections move as rigid parts. Their floors, stairs and load-bearing ribs stay together; the joints between separate sections can slide, unseat or topple. This deliberately trades fine fracture for fewer moving bodies. Landscape, props and environment remain presentation art.
