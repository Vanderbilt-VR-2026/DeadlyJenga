DEMO MENU AND ARSENAL EXPANSION - 2026-10-24

MENUS
The desktop Demo menu now has Personal Controls, Bot Controls, Game Controls and
Misc. Each tab has a category dropdown. Equipment lists can be filtered to all,
powers, ranged or melee. Advanced mode-rule fields are grouped by round/scoring,
combat/health, movement/traversal and world/disasters. Existing options remain in
their owning panels: character/third-person/spectating, survivor and hand bots,
voice, jaguars, mode/maps/disasters, resets, scaffold delivery, powerup weights,
networking, audio, comfort, stamina, HUD/standings and control help.
The VR root has the same four groups. Item grants and random-spawn weights are
paged so every new item is accessible with the gaze/trigger menu.

USE
Personal Controls > Equipment: grant one item to yourself.
Bot Controls > Bot equipment: give every survivor player and bot an item for this
life, or now and every respawn. Stop respawn grants preserves held equipment.
Game Controls > Ground pickups: spawn a collectible. Powerup spawning opens the
weighted random-spawn settings, now containing all equipment types.
Desktop: V draws/holsters; click attacks; hold for automatic/continuous/charging
weapons; R changes supported modes. VR: dominant trigger draws/fires; trigger
near holster stows; other trigger changes supported modes; support grip aims
heavy two-hand weapons. VR melee uses hand movement; claws support both hands.

NEW WEAPONS
Rocket launcher: shoulder launcher inspired by the supplied references, travelling
rocket, splash damage and push, back sling when stowed.
Dual Uzis: paired hand models and alternating automatic fire.
Chain gun: rotating barrel cluster, spin-up, sustained fire.
Grenade launcher: ballistic projectiles and blast damage; bots calculate elevation.
Wrist laser: forearm-mounted emitter, continuous beam/damage while held.
Needle drill: cordless-drill form, one-second spin-up, visible metal needles.
Stock pistol: C96-style pistol with wooden shoulder stock, full auto / three-round burst.
Saw staff: spinning large serrated wheel on a handle.
Barbed bat: wooden bat with coiled wire and protruding nails.
Chainsaw: contact cutting / one-second charged flying-wheel mode.
Laser claws: three short blades on each hand.
Flaming marshmallow: white marshmallow, flame shapes and stick melee.
Titan laser axe: large blade / wide laser mode.
PvP dagger: stronger fallback melee, enabled by default for CTF, King of the Hill,
Block Control and Last Team Standing. Mystery and Infection retain their existing
role behavior. Default dagger is a mode rule, requires punching enabled, does not
consume/drop an inventory item, and stays usable during speed/jump powers. Timed
powers retain swap/drop/expiry behavior and survive authority restoration.

PICKUPS
A translucent irregular static cloud replaces the solid beacon. Weapon previews
use geometry/material data from the same character models, scaled to fit inside.
Jump is bright green; speed is bright blue. Armor uses the actual armor overlay
geometry. Models rotate for readability. Visuals add no gameplay colliders.

IMPLEMENTATION
Approved additive Core changes: 14 equipment IDs appended after the old IDs,
shared equipment specification/model data, projectile and charge/trigger data,
IHeldEquipmentRuntime, optional defaultDagger rule, and activePower data allowing
fallback melee alongside a timed movement power. Existing IDs and commands stay.
GameSession/Modes owns authoritative damage, charge/cooldowns, burst limits,
projectiles/explosions, pickup policy, schema checks and restoration. Projectiles
are capped at 64, use swept nonalloc queries, start moving after their birth frame,
check initial overlaps, and stop at blocking geometry. Held-trigger heartbeats
expire safely. Presentation owns effects/audio/menus. Characters owns input,
hand/holster placement, model geometry and generic bot weapon execution. New models
are combined by color/material and cache meshes, retaining moving assemblies.
