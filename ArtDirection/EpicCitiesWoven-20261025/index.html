DEADLY JENGA · CORRECTED BUILT MAPS

A lower city woven through the skyline

The previous pass left tower clusters separated by broad bare spaces. These revisions add physical folded terraces, open market arcades, split courtyard pavilions, branching mesa walks and stepped porticos between them. Low roofs connect to neighboring low roofs and tower floors; ground passages remain beneath and between the structures.

Choose Epic connected cities in the lobby or demo map picker. Existing IDs are retained; these are revised versions of the same ten maps.

Download the comparison gallery and evidence

All pictures below are actual Unity game captures. Each before/after pair uses the same camera. The 16-second wave exposure is a targeted geometry and behavior check with 12 bots; it is not the earlier full-wave 32-bot benchmark, and its timings should not be compared with that run. Headset, live multiplayer and competitive mode balance remain unverified.
