Packed Shapes removes 832 GameObjects while preserving all 633 independent physical bodies and all 1,049 structural collision shapes. It reduced median frame time by 33.6% idle, 23.7% with bots and 20.6% with bots plus tsunami in this run. This is about 26% higher equivalent FPS under the wave, not a claim of 26% less total physics work or a guaranteed Quest speedup. Large 1.1-second p90 stalls remain.
Surface Mesh has the same GameObject reduction and further removes 416 player-collision shapes, but measured 188.25ms under the wave versus Packed's 124.26ms and the baseline's 156.42ms. Reject it as the preferred optimization on this evidence. Its smaller shape count did not produce better stress performance. Additional mesh query/update cost is a hypothesis, not a measured marker attribution.

RECOMMENDATION
Use Festival City - Packed Shapes as the preferred experimental copy; keep the original and Surface Mesh copy available for reproducible comparison. Do not silently replace the original or claim Quest readiness. Next investigate eliminating/localizing duplicated character collision, with proper moving-support and overlap-recovery tests, then profile room rendering and compiled bot navigation. Larger welded/static-release assemblies are separate gameplay/framework decisions.
