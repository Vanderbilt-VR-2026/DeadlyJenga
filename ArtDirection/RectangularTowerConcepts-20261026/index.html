Atrium starting arithmetic: 8 levels × (4 posts + 4 floor slabs + 1 ramp) = 72 bodies, plus cover, landings and roof pieces. Split-level starts around 48 support segments + 32 floor blocks + 16 ramp flights = 96 before cover and roof. Hollow stack starts around 8 × (6 wall blocks + 2 floor slabs + 1 ramp) = 72 before landings and roof. Use simple box colliders for cuboids. Every additional compound collider still has a cost.

1 · Atrium frame

Four separate slab edges make a gallery around a central void. Short stacked supports allow individual bays to lose support. Keep slab edges inside the support envelope and preserve a clear climbing face. Rendered small overhangs should be corrected during construction.

2 · Split-level switchback — recommended first prototype

Half-level platforms create shorter transitions and different sightlines within one rectangular tower. Broad landings provide places to fight or hide. Prototype a two-storey section first: verify ramp headroom, bot routing, contact stability and useful partial collapse before stacking it higher.

3 · Hollow block stack

Broad wall blocks replace rows of little bricks while leaving large door and window gaps. Stagger openings without losing vertical bearing overlap. This gives stronger enclosed/open contrast, but fallen walls must not seal every route.

All three need Unity physics, traversal and stress testing. The generated ramps and collapse insets express intent, not measured engineering. No rigidbody joints, magnetism or guaranteed staged failure are assumed. No gameplay files changed for these concepts.
