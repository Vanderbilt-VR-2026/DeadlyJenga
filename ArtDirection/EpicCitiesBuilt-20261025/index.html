What was built

Independently moving multi-floor sections, real open stair slots, clear climbing ribs, bearing rafts, loose bridges, physical intermediate supports, and one continuous shore-matched terrain collider per island. Caldera, Lantern Ravine and Rising Spine have actual ground elevation. Bookfall has three hinged wings with receiving feet and physical catches; Three Hearts includes the drop-stop assembly.

The images were interpreted as playable structures rather than copied as exact engineering drawings. Multi-floor cassettes move as units; their individual stairs, piers and floor strips do not each fracture separately. Some proposed bridges remain ground streets when a safe bearing route cannot fit. Heights and spans were adapted to verified collision clearance.

Verification and limits

  • All ten passed native geometry, settling and full-size ascent sampling; a focused relay follow-up is retained separately from the earlier full report.
  • Production reports below cover real survivor motors, 32 bots, a default-force tsunami, stronger demolition, layout roundtrip and reset.
  • No Core contract, global solver setting, package or gameplay scene was changed. Existing maps were preserved.
  • Mode-specific competitive balancing and real Quest/multiplayer sessions remain follow-up work. A large playable map is not automatically balanced for every population and mode.
