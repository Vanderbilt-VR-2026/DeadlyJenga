# Jaguar enemy coat

The enemy retains the normal Candy avatar, its 44 bones, controller hands, movement, health and hitboxes. The existing `PantherHead`, `PantherClaws` and `PantherTail` appearance bits now add the jaguar coat and features to that avatar. Existing saved/network appearance data keeps working. The legacy `panther` enemy ID is retained, and spawning also accepts `jaguar`.

The coat uses an original seamless 512px rosette texture, with broken scalloped rings and central spots, plus a subtle normal map for the short fur. It is golden rather than tinted black by the old enemy script. Rounded ears, a broad skull and cheeks, double cream muzzle, charcoal nose/lips/whisker pores, amber eyes, fangs, cream chest, muscular shoulders/haunches, padded paws, curved claws and a spotted moving tail are cosmetic additions on the existing bones. They introduce no colliders or gameplay abilities. First-person rendering conceals the head/chest/tail while retaining the hands and shadows.

The fur textures are shared across all jaguars, use mipmaps and a 512px cap, and are loaded by Resources name. Meshes/materials are cached. An authored Resources material retains the normal-map shader variant in player builds. The coat uses URP Lit. Presentation's health shader now also samples the original coat UVs and normal map, and its observer preserves source textures, tiling and material properties when installing the health effect. Ordinary untextured avatars retain a white texture multiplied by their existing color. `CandyJaguarAppearance.CoatTexture` and `PieceCount` expose read-only validation data. No network format change is needed.

Jaguar sound is owned by Presentation. `CandyJaguarSounds` supplies three original deterministic variants each for purr/breath, chuff, growl, alert, attack, hurt, death, paw step and landing. Synthesized throat harmonics, irregular rasp and short padded Foley match the existing candy sound palette; they are designed creature sounds, not wildlife recordings. Bank-authored clips can override individual cues. These use the ordinary Character bus, master settings, distance culling, per-owner cooldowns and bounded shared voice pool. Ambient voices are staggered globally and per enemy. The original human grunts/steps are suppressed for enemies. Typed `EnemyActions` announces alerts and accepted strikes; the session also observes accepted damage and elimination. The authority publishes those positional combat events through Core, and Presentation consumes that history on all peers.

Remote NPC movement, paw landings and ambience are observed from existing actor state. Combat cues use the separate EnemyActionFrame history because NPCs are intentionally absent from the participant scoreboard. Up to 64 ordered events live for 1.2 game seconds and retain the archetype, actor identity and position even after removal. The first snapshot and changed epochs are silent baselines; repeated snapshots do not replay combat. Authority restore preserves the epoch and highest sequence while clearing stale transient sounds. Unknown future enemy archetypes are safely ignored by the jaguar audio observer.

For targeted explicit Play Mode checks, DevTools `JaguarSuiteChecks.ValidateAppearance(rig)` checks a spawned enemy shell/rig/material/mesh, `ValidateHealthFrames(rig, completed)` waits six actual frames and checks a health-enabled match keeps the coat, and `ValidateSounds()` checks all 27 variants for finite bounded PCM, audible peaks, fades, mix buses and voice limits. Actual headset sound balance, CPU/GPU budget and subjective listening still require device testing.
