# Christmas Candy Town artwork

Selected models and colormap: **Kenney — Holiday Kit 2.0**, created 11 December 2024.

Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/holiday-kit

License: Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0). The original license is retained at ThirdParty/KenneyHoliday/License.txt. Commercial use and modification are permitted; attribution is optional. Models were selected from the original OBJ distribution; the entire package was not imported. No third-party scripts or packages are included.

The town's gingerbread architecture, collision layout, signs, candy roads, frosting details and material variants are authored in the project. ChristmasTownTools creates the Presentation visual prefab and combined meshes from these components. Shared URP materials provide a glossy, colorful finish consistent with the project's candy scenery. Surface textures are created deterministically by the editor tool; the winter sky and shelter-aware snow are project shaders. No AI-generated imagery or paid assets are used.
