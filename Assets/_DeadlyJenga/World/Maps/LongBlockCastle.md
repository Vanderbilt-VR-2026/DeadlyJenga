# Long Block Castle

Select **Candy Castle · Long Blocks and Gaps** in the lobby map selector. The original Candy Castle is unchanged.

Blocks measure 9 × 2 × 2 metres instead of 6 × 2 × 2. Every adjacent outer tower has **3 metres of clear space**, increased from 0.5 metres. Wall towers have 14 or 16 courses; corner towers have 18. Descending rooftop crossings therefore drop two or four courses (4 or 8 metres), rather than six courses at the corners. Each course is one block high.

Spacing targets the default seated VR speed of 2.5 m/s, gravity of 18 m/s² and 1.82 m jump height. Jump near the edge onto an intact lower block. The actual player motor crossed the isolated full-block test gap with two through five courses of descent. Walking off failed at every one-through-five-course drop at that speed. The test uses static full block colliders; intact tower stability and live map loading are checked separately. Desktop defaults to 4.6 m/s and is more forgiving; faster movement and powerups can change which gaps need a jump. A drawn rifle still disables jumping. Uphill rooftop crossings require climbing or a different route.

The footprint is 57 × 57 metres, with a 61 × 61 metre foundation and matching disaster bounds. Island coverage, spawn surfaces and replicated layouts derive from these settings. The central keep is unchanged; spreading the outer ring also increases its distance from the walls, so this outer-tower jump tuning does not guarantee a direct keep-to-wall jump.

The longer blocks open the hollow centres of the alternating tower courses. The copied block retains its physics and visible gray-box fallback without Presentation art.
