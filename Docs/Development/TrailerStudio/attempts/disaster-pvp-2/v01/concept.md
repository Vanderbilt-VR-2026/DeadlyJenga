# Stage 1 concept: Disaster PvP 2, "Last One Standing (For Now)"

## Images people will remember (seven candidates)
1. Your own glove, in first person, slamming into a gorilla's face and sending him sailing off the summit. (chosen, S02)
2. A rock landing between two friends mid shoot-out, both blown off their block, seen from a metre away. (chosen, S04)
3. Spinning round to find a dragon charging you down a 6 m block, and countering him off the end. (chosen, S05-S06)
4. Looking up from the summit as a meteor the size of the tower fills your whole view. (chosen, S08)
5. Two survivors riding a tilting block, still punching. (rejected: riding a tilting block sinks the rider; untested)
6. A tornado sucking a whole duel off the top. (rejected: the tornado's timing on a survivor is not directable)
7. A tsunami crest rising behind a fistfight. (rejected: the hazard tsunami picks its own side; spent by example D)

## Chosen concept
- **Logline:** On the summit of a tower under a meteor storm, you punch out the gorilla in front of you and the dragon behind you, and win the top just in time for the sky to land on it.
- **Concept type:** mayhem (PvP first at 2.5 s, the meteor storm's first hit at 4.1 s).
- **Hero:** the plain yellow survivor with red spiky hair and the boxing glove (`hero`), on the summit the whole cut.
- **Opening:** staged. The meteor threat is known to the audience first (rocks streak across the sky behind the brawl in S01) before its first full hit in S03; the planet killer is anticipated by the hero's reaction (S07) before its first full view (S08).
- **Shape:** montage, 9 body-and-title shots, three first-person shots (34 % of the body).
- **Arc in three words:** brawl, win, doom.
- **Turn:** the hero wins every fight, and winning the summit is exactly what puts him under the planet killer.
- **Title:** "DEADLY JENGA" on the title hit (f359), tagline "WIN THE TOP. LOSE THE SKY." (new; not an example's).

## Two rejected concepts (each differs from the chosen in at least two of shape, opening, payoff, ending)
1. **"Duel in the Funnel"**: a single take that orbits two gloved survivors as a tornado eats the tower around them; cold open on the funnel; payoff both sucked up together; ends on a wide of the funnel. Differs in shape (single take), opening (cold open), payoff and ending. Rejected: the tornado's pull on a survivor is unconfirmed and not timeable, so the key hit could not be placed close.
2. **"Down the Face"**: first-person-led (over 60 %) fall from the summit after being punched, meteors passing as the hero tumbles 38 m to the castle wall; staged on the punch; payoff the hero landing alive; ends on him looking up. Differs in shape (POV-led), payoff and ending. Rejected: a tumbling POV is unreadable for most of its length, and it repeats disaster-pvp-1's tumble.

## What the Studio gives this plan
Driven actors (StudioPerformer: goTo, look, face, punch with the real glove, fire), the alive layer, aimed meteors and the aimed planet killer, the first-person camera with own hands and glove, reaction and look-at layers, and a new `hide` layer (added for this attempt) for the respawns. Missing: an unarmed punch cue (the glove is used), a person in the headset (waived), real eliminations (the hidden respawn plus the splash stand in).
