# Material swatches and small objects

## Material swatches

A swatch is a flat photo of one material (brick, stone, grass). Later it
becomes a repeating texture for ground and walls.

Shoot each of these at least once, more if campus has several kinds:
**red brick, limestone, other stone, paving (each kind), asphalt, bark
(two or three tree types), grass, gravel or mulch.**

How:
1. **Even light:** a fully shaded patch or an overcast day. No sun spots,
   no shadow of you or the phone in the frame.
2. **Square-on:** phone parallel to the surface. For the ground, hold it
   flat above, pointing straight down. For walls, face the wall straight.
3. **Distance:** about 1 m, so the photo covers about 1 m of material.
   For brick, make sure several full rows and joints show.
4. **Gray card** in the corner of the first shot.
5. **Scale:** a ruler or tape measure in the first shot too (a coin or
   your phone case works if you forgot it). Say what it is in your notes.
6. Take a second shot **without** the card and ruler, same spot.
7. Then 4 to 6 overlapping shots of the same material nearby (step to the
   side each time), so a bigger patch can be stitched.

Write down where each swatch was taken (building and side).

## Small objects

Benches, lamp posts, signs, bins, bike racks, fountains, statues.

### Option A: orbit photos (any phone)
- Walk a full circle around the object, **one photo every 10 to 15
  degrees** (about 24 to 36 shots per circle).
- Do two circles: one at chest height, one higher or lower, tilting the
  phone toward the object.
- Fill the frame with the object, but keep all of it in view.
- Gray card first frame, exposure locked, as usual.
- Signs: shoot them, but real sign text and logos are not used in the
  released game (they become blank signs).

### Option B: a phone scanning app
These make a rough 3D model on the phone. Save the original photos too,
if the app allows it.

| App | Cost (checked 2026-11-19) | Notes |
|-----|---------------------------|-------|
| **Scaniverse** (iPhone; check the Android store) | Free tier; paid plans exist | Processes on the phone. Free tier is for personal scans |
| **KIRI Engine** | Free basic plan | Up to 150 photos per scan, unlimited exports; LiDAR scans free. Pro is $79.99 per year (optional) |
| **Polycam** | Free tier | LiDAR mode free on iPhones with LiDAR; photo mode has a limited free number. Check current limits |

LiDAR scanning needs an iPhone Pro or iPad Pro with a LiDAR sensor. Free
limits change often; check in the app before a big session.

**Privacy:** these apps may upload scans to their cloud. Only scan
objects with no people in view, and still keep the original photos for
the archive.
