# Weather, light and privacy

## Best conditions

- **Overcast (cloudy, bright, no rain) is best.** Soft light means no hard
  shadows, so the textures can be relit in the game later.
- **Mid-day** (about 10 am to 3 pm) gives the most even light.
- **Avoid:** rain and wet surfaces (they shine and change color), low
  sun at sunrise or sunset (long shadows, orange light), fog, wind that
  shakes trees in front of walls.
- **Winter leaf-off** (bare trees) shows much more of each facade. Many
  facades are best shot between late November and March.
- Snow on roofs or ground: skip that day.

## Sunny day? Use the shade-side rule

On a sunny day, shoot each wall **when it is in shade**, not in sun.

| Wall faces | In shade (roughly) |
|------------|--------------------|
| North | All day (best wall on a sunny day) |
| East | Afternoon |
| West | Morning |
| South | Rarely; save it for an overcast day |

The shot list also gives a best time for each facade.

## People and privacy

- **Public outdoor areas only.** Sidewalks, lawns, plazas, paths.
- **Do not enter buildings** or go past signs, gates or fences.
- **Do not photograph into windows.** Shoot the facade, not what is
  behind the glass. If someone is at a window, wait or skip it.
- **Wait for people to pass** before each shot. Nobody should fill the
  frame.
- If someone asks, be friendly and short: "I'm a student making a 3D
  model of campus buildings for a game project. I'm only photographing
  the buildings, and faces get blurred automatically." If they would
  rather not be in a photo, delete that shot in front of them.
- **Faces and license plates are blurred automatically** in the copies
  the computer works on. **The original photos never leave your PC** (and
  your own backup drive). Never post or share originals.

## Campus rules

- **No drones.** Students may not fly drones on campus.
- **No restricted areas:** construction zones, loading docks, roofs,
  anything marked staff only.
- **Hospital and medical center zones:** stay on public sidewalks. Do not
  photograph patients, ambulance bays, or entrances while people are
  there. When in doubt, skip that building.
- If campus security or staff ask you to stop, stop, and note the place
  so the plan can work around it.
