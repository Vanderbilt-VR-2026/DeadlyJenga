# iPhone setup

Menu names move a little between iOS versions. If one is missing, look
nearby in Settings > Camera.

## 1. File format

**Pro models (iPhone 12 Pro and later Pro):** use **Apple ProRAW**. RAW
means the photo keeps all the sensor data, so color and brightness can be
fixed later.
- Settings > Camera > Formats > turn on **Apple ProRAW & Resolution
  Control**.
- Choose **48 MP** ProRAW if your phone offers it (iPhone 14 Pro and
  later). 48 MP works only on the main camera at 1x.
- In the Camera app, tap **RAW** at the top so it is on (not crossed out).
- Settings > Camera > **Preserve Settings** > turn on **Apple ProRAW**, so
  it stays on after you close the app.

**Non-Pro iPhones (no RAW in the stock app):** Settings > Camera >
Formats > **High Efficiency** (HEIC). Use the highest resolution offered.

## 2. Turn these off

- **Live Photos**: the circle icon at the top of the Camera app. Off.
- **Night mode**: the moon icon. If it turns yellow, tap it and set Off.
- **Filters / Photographic Styles**: set to Standard (no style).
- **Macro auto-switch**: Settings > Camera > Macro Control on, then keep
  the flower icon off in the Camera app. Otherwise the phone may jump to
  the ultra-wide lens up close.
- **Zoom**: stay on **1x**. Never pinch to zoom. That is digital zoom; it
  only crops.
- **Flash**: off.
- HDR: newer iPhones blend exposures automatically and give no switch.
  ProRAW still keeps the most data, which is why it is preferred.

## 3. Keep location on

- Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > Camera > **While
  Using the App**, and **Precise Location** on.
- Keep **Airplane mode off**. GPS works better with cell and Wi-Fi on.

## 4. Lock exposure and focus (stock Camera app)

1. Point at the wall (not the sky).
2. Touch and hold on the wall until **AE/AF LOCK** shows in yellow.
3. Take the whole pass without tapping the screen again. A tap unlocks it.
4. New wall or new light: lock again.

The stock app cannot lock white balance (the color tint). With ProRAW
that is fine: the gray card fixes it later. With HEIC, avoid walking from
shade into sun in one set.

## 5. Optional manual camera app

**Halide Mark III** (App Store). It can set shutter speed, ISO and white
balance by hand, and shoots RAW. It is **paid**: $19.99 per year, $9.99
per month, or $69.99 once, with a 7-day trial on the yearly plan. Check
current price. You do not need it; the stock app with ProRAW is enough.

## 6. Storage math

| Format | Size per photo (Apple) | Photos per GB |
|--------|------------------------|---------------|
| ProRAW 48 MP | about 75 MB | about 13 |
| ProRAW 12 MP | about 25 MB | about 40 |
| HEIC (Apple: RAW is 10 to 12x bigger) | about 6 to 8 MB at 48 MP | about 130 to 160 |

A hero building can take 300 to 600 photos. At 48 MP ProRAW that is 25
to 45 GB. Free space and copy to the PC after each session.

## 7. Getting photos off the iPhone

- Settings > Photos > Transfer to Mac or PC > **Keep Originals**.
- If iCloud Photos is on, choose **Download and Keep Originals**, not
  "Optimize iPhone Storage".
- Plug into the PC by cable, unlock the phone, tap **Trust**, then copy
  from the phone's DCIM folder in File Explorer.
- First time: check that the `.DNG` files arrived and are the size the
  table above says. If not, tell the builder chat before deleting
  anything from the phone.
